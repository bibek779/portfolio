About Me

I am Gopal Sharma, a PhD student at the Graduate School of Interdisciplinary Science and Engineering, Kyushu University, Japan. My research journey began in aerospace and mechanical engineering but later pivoted toward game theory — the focus of my PhD project. Originally from Nepal, I am currently based in Japan, working on interdisciplinary approaches that bridge engineering, mathematics, and artificial intelligence.
